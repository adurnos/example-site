Privacy

Demo page

Rose City Plumbing Co. is a fictional sample business used to demonstrate a local plumbing website. Its contact details and services are placeholders, not a real business offer.

Contact form

The sample contact form is configured to use FormSubmit with a clearly marked placeholder recipient. Before launch, replace the placeholder with the real business inbox in the form action. FormSubmit may process the information entered in the form to deliver the inquiry. Review FormSubmit’s privacy practices before using the service. Its first-use email verification must be completed by the business inbox owner.

External resources

The demo loads fonts from Google Fonts and photographs from Unsplash. Those services may receive standard technical information from your browser when the resources load. Review their privacy information for details.

Real business websites

Before using this design for a real business, replace all sample contact details, connect a secure form service if needed, and publish a privacy notice that accurately describes the live site and its service providers.
