What should I do if a pipe is leaking?

If it’s safe and you know where your home’s main water shutoff is, turn it off. Move belongings away from the water, then call us to talk through next steps. If water is near electrical equipment, keep clear and contact the appropriate emergency service.

Do you offer estimates before starting work?

We’ll discuss what you’re noticing and explain the next step before work begins. The right estimate depends on what’s going on, so we’ll talk it through with you first.

Can I call if I don’t know what’s wrong?

Absolutely. Describe what you’re seeing, like a slow drain, a new sound, or a water stain, and we’ll help you figure out what kind of visit makes sense.

Do you work outside Portland?

We serve Portland and select nearby communities. Call and tell us your neighborhood; we’ll confirm whether it’s in our service area.
