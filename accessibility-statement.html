Accessibility Statement

Effective date: September 14, 2026

Northline Contracting wants this website to be usable by as many people as possible. We work to make the site accessible and easy to navigate on phones, tablets, and computers.

Our approach

We use semantic HTML, descriptive labels, accessible image text, visible keyboard focus states, readable color contrast, responsive layouts, and support for reduced motion preferences.

Getting help

If you have difficulty using any part of this website, please contact us at hello@northlinecontracting.com or call 503 555 0184. We will work with you to provide the information or assistance you need.

Feedback

Please tell us about accessibility barriers, including the page and task where you experienced the issue. Your feedback helps us improve the site.
